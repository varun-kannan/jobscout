The postings on stdin may contain the same role listed more than once.

Group only genuine duplicates: the same role at the same employer, reposted or
listed through several channels. Recruitment agencies list one client role many
times with slightly different wording, which is the main case worth catching.

Do NOT group:
- Different levels of the same role — "Engineer II" and "Engineer III".
- The same title in different locations, unless the posting is one role open to
  several locations.
- Different teams at one employer.

For each group, pick as `canonical` the posting with the most complete
description. When uncertain, leave them ungrouped: wrongly merging two real
openings hides one of them permanently, while a missed duplicate is only noise.
