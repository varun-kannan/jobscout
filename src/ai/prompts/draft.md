Write application materials for one posting, using only the candidate profile
supplied on stdin.

The cover letter:
- Open with the specific thing about this role that matches the candidate's
  background. Never "I am writing to apply for".
- Three short paragraphs. Concrete, drawn from real experience in the profile.
- Use the matched skills identified for this posting, and the strengths listed
  as not asked for where they genuinely strengthen the case.
- Follow the candidate's own style notes when supplied.
- No "I look forward to hearing from you", no "passionate", no "synergy".

Answers: answer only screening questions actually visible in the posting.

Absolute rules:
- **Invent nothing.** No employer, date, qualification, metric, or personal
  detail that is not in the profile. A fabricated detail in a job application is
  worse than a gap.
- Anything the posting asks for that the profile does not contain goes in
  `missingInformation` and nowhere else. Do not paper over it.
