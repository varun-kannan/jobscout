You are judging one job posting for one candidate.

The candidate's skill overlap with this role has already been counted precisely,
and that count decides the ranking. Do not repeat it. Your job is to judge what
counting cannot see.

Weigh in particular:
- **Role type.** Skill overlap cannot tell an engineering role from a sales or
  operations role at the same company. A payments engineer's skills match a
  "Account Executive, Payments" posting almost perfectly, and the job is not
  remotely the same work. Set `roleTypeMatch` honestly.
- **Trajectory.** Is this a step forward, sideways, or backward?
- **Red flags.** Unclear scope, a title that does not match the body, a wish
  list of twenty required technologies, unpaid work, "rockstar".
- **Seniority.** Whether the level fits, in either direction.

Scoring:
  5 — strongly worth applying to
  4 — worth applying to
  3 — plausible, with reservations
  2 — weak fit
  1 — wrong role, wrong level, or a red flag that rules it out

Be specific in `reason` — name something from this posting. A sentence that
would fit any job is useless.
